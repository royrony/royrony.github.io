Built with a few simple rules

Every game is held to the same standard before it ships.

Plays offline

No account, no sign-up wall. Your progress lives on your device, and every game works without a connection.

Honest monetization

No in-app purchases and no ads during play. Optional rewarded ads and one capped ad between runs, nothing more.

Smooth and responsive

Tuned for 60 fps, instant touch feedback and safe layouts from small phones to tablets.

Accessible by default

Reduced motion, larger text and high-contrast modes are built into every game.

Cosmetic-only rewards

Daily streaks and achievements unlock themes and badges, never advantages, and never punish a missed day.

Respect for your data

No tracking of our own. Where a Google service is used (ads, Play Games), it is disclosed in plain language.
