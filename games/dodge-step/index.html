Dodge Step

Hold still. Slide at the last beat. A one-thumb lane dodger scored on composure and late dodges: the later you move, the more you earn.

Calm hands, sharp timing

Shapes fall down three lanes. You only get a small step sideways, so the skill is waiting. Staying still while danger is on screen builds Composure; dodging at the very last moment scores a Clutch. Move too early, or twitch, and you leave points on the table.

Late-dodge scoring

Every dodge is graded Early, Good, Perfect or Clutch based on how late you moved. Clutch dodges are worth ten times an Early one.

Composure meter

Holding still while a threat falls fills your composure for a bigger multiplier. Twitching costs it.

Combo up to x7

Chain graded dodges to build a combo multiplier. Miss the rhythm and it resets.

Ten rising tiers

Speed and pressure climb every 300 points. Survive tier 10 for two seconds to earn You Win.

Continue once

Reached Game Over? Watch one optional short ad to continue the same run, with your score and level kept.

Three themes

Midnight, Neon and Sunset palettes, saved between sessions, plus large text, high contrast and reduced motion.

How to play

A run takes two to five minutes. The whole game is one thumb.

  1. Tap to startTap anywhere on the ready screen. A short countdown, then shapes begin to fall.
  2. Wait for itStay still while a shape falls toward you. Your Composure meter fills, and your multiplier grows with it.
  3. Slide lateSwipe or tap the left or right half of the screen to step one lane. The closer the shape, the better the grade.
  4. Chain and climbKeep dodging to build a combo and rise through ten tiers. Hit a shape and the run ends.

Controls: swipe left/right, or tap the left/right half of the screen. On a keyboard use the arrow keys or A / D, Space or P to pause, R to restart. One lane per move, no wrap-around.

Leaderboard & achievements

Dodge Step is connected to Google Play Games Services. Sign in once and your best score is posted to the Top Score leaderboard, so you can see how you rank against friends and other players. Eight achievements unlock as you play.

  • Optional: the game works exactly the same if you never sign in.
  • Synced: scores and achievements earned offline or signed out are sent the next time you are signed in.
  • Where to find it: open the game menu and tap Leaderboard or Achievements (these appear on Android when Play Games is available).
  • Your data: Google receives your score, achievement progress and Play Games profile name. See the privacy policy.

FAQ

Is Dodge Step free?

Yes. There are no in-app purchases. The game shows optional rewarded ads (for the one-time Continue) and a single capped ad between runs, never during play.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game plays fully offline. Internet is only used for ads and, if you choose to sign in, Google Play Games.

Will my progress be lost if I uninstall?

Progress, best score and settings are stored on your device and are removed when you uninstall. Play Games leaderboard scores and achievements stay with your Google account.

Why doesn't the Leaderboard button show for me?

The Leaderboard and Achievements buttons appear on Android devices where Google Play Games is available. Make sure Google Play Games is installed and you are signed in.
