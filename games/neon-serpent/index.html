Neon Serpent: Snake Arcade

The classic snake game, recharged with arcade neon glow. Steer a glowing serpent across a midnight grid, swallow the orbs and push your high score higher.

Get it on Google PlayHow to play
FreePlays offlineNo in-app purchasesEveryonePhones & tablets

Classic snake, modern feel

Fast, focused, one-thumb arcade action wrapped in a synthwave board, glowing food, punchy sound and a screen shake that lands on every bite.

One-thumb control

Swipe anywhere to steer. Quick-turn buffering means fast inputs never skip or reverse. An on-screen D-pad, arrow keys and WASD are also supported.

Combo system

Chain bites to build a score multiplier up to x7. Break the rhythm and it resets.

Rising speed

The grid tightens with every orb. Length, speed and food count stay on screen.

Three neon themes

Neon Circuit, Sunset Drive and Mint Arcade, saved between sessions.

Best score, always saved

Your personal best is stored the moment a run ends. No account, no internet, no waiting.

Made for everyone

Safe-area aware one-handed portrait play, large text, high contrast and a reduced-motion mode.

How to play

  1. SteerSwipe, press an arrow key or use the D-pad to change direction.
  2. EatSwallow the glowing orbs to grow your snake and score.
  3. ComboChain bites quickly to build a multiplier of up to x7.
  4. SurviveNever hit the wall, and never cross your own tail.

FAQ

Is Neon Serpent free?

Yes. No in-app purchases. Optional rewarded ads are the only ads, and they never appear during play.

Does it work offline?

Yes, fully. No account and no internet needed.

Is it safe for kids?

The app is rated Everyone, but it is not designed for children under 13 and is not part of the Play Families program because it can show ads.

How do I report a bug?

Email ronyroy222@gmail.com with your device model and what happened.
